# Go-To-Market Plan

## Exact target user

The target user is an engineering manager or CTO at a seed to Series A startup with 5 to 30 employees. They are technical enough to recognize tool names like Cursor and GitHub Copilot but too busy to audit their own subscriptions. They approved the AI tool budget 6 months ago and have not revisited it since. They feel mild guilt every month when the bills come in but lack a benchmark to know if they are overspending.

Job title: Engineering Manager, VP Engineering, or CTO
Company stage: Seed to Series A, 5 to 30 people
Geography: US, UK, India

## What they Google or scroll through right before wanting this tool

- "cursor vs github copilot 2025"
- "is chatgpt team worth it for small team"
- "claude pro vs team plan difference"
- "how much should a startup spend on AI tools"
- Reddit posts complaining about rising SaaS bills
- Hacker News threads about AI tool fatigue

- r/ExperiencedDevs and r/startups on Reddit
- Indie Hackers community and forums
- Slack communities: Rands Leadership Slack, SaaStr Community, Online Geniuses
- X/Twitter following accounts like @swyx, @levelsio, @dvassallo
- Newsletter communities: TLDR, The Pragmatic Engineer, Lenny's Newsletter readers

## How to get the first 100 users in 30 days with zero paid budget

Week 1: Post a Show HN — "Show HN: I built a free tool to audit your AI tool spend." Write a genuine post explaining the problem, the tool, and what it found when I ran it on my own stack. HN drives high-quality technical traffic and the tool is exactly what that audience cares about.

Week 2: Post in r/ExperiencedDevs and r/startups with a specific angle — "We audited 50 AI tool stacks and here is what we found." Use real anonymized data from Week 1 audits to make it credible. Reddit drives volume if the post is specific and not promotional.

Week 3: Cold DM 20 engineering managers on X who have recently tweeted about AI tools or SaaS costs. Not a pitch — a genuine "I built this thing, would love your feedback" message with a link. Founders and EMs respond to builders.

Week 4: Write a short blog post titled "The 5 most common ways startups overpay for AI tools" and post it on dev.to and Hashnode. Each tip links to the tool naturally. Dev.to has strong SEO and the post will rank for long-tail queries within weeks.

## The unfair distribution channel

Credex already has relationships with AI tool vendors and resellers. A co-marketing agreement where Cursor or Anthropic mentions SpendSmart AI in their onboarding emails or pricing pages would drive thousands of qualified users overnight. No other individual builder has this access — only Credex does.

- 200 to 400 audits completed from the HN post
- 30 to 60 email captures from the lead form
- 5 to 10 users sharing their audit URL on X or Slack
