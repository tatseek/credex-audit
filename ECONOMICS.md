# Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits. A typical deal involves a startup buying $500 to $5000 of AI credits at a 20 to 40 percent discount versus retail. Credex margin on a deal is estimated at 15 to 25 percent of the credit value.

Conservative estimate: average deal size $1000, Credex margin 20 percent = $200 gross profit per converted customer. If the customer rebills quarterly, LTV over 12 months = $800.

## CAC at each channel

- Hacker News Show HN post: $0 paid, ~4 hours of writing time. If 300 audits and 2 percent convert to consultation and 50 percent of consultations close: 3 customers. CAC = 0 dollars.
- Reddit posts: $0 paid, ~2 hours per post. Similar conversion funnel. CAC = 0 dollars.
- Cold DM on X: $0 paid, ~5 hours for 20 DMs. If 2 respond and 1 converts: CAC = 0 dollars.
- Blog post SEO: $0 paid, ~6 hours writing. Compounds over time. CAC = 0 dollars.
- If Credex ever runs paid ads: estimated CPC $2 to $5 for dev tool keywords, conversion rate from ad to audit 10 percent, audit to lead 15 percent, lead to close 10 percent. CAC = approximately $130 to $330 per customer.

## Conversion funnel

- Visitor lands on SpendSmart AI: 100 percent
- Completes audit: 40 percent (low friction, no login)
- Submits email: 20 percent of those who complete audit
- Books Credex consultation (high savings cases only): 10 percent of email captures
- Purchases credits: 50 percent of consultations

Overall: 100 visitors = 40 audits = 8 leads = 0.8 consultations = 0.4 customers

At $200 gross profit per customer: need 250 visitors per customer. At $0 CAC from organic channels, this is profitable from day one.

## What would have to be true for $1M ARR in 18 months

Target: $1M ARR = $83,000 per month revenue to Credex

At $200 gross profit per customer and average rebill of 4 times per year: need 1,250 active customers.

To get 1,250 customers in 18 months: need roughly 70 new customers per month by month 12.

At 0.4 percent visitor to customer rate: need 17,500 visitors per month by month 12.

This is achievable if:
- Show HN drives 2,000 to 5,000 visitors in month 1
- SEO compounds to 5,000 organic visitors per month by month 6
- Credex vendor co-marketing drives 5,000 to 10,000 visitors per month by month 9
- Word of mouth from shareable audit URLs contributes 10 to 20 percent of traffic

The $1M ARR scenario requires the vendor co-marketing channel to work. Without it, organic growth alone reaches roughly $200k to $400k ARR in 18 months which is still a strong outcome for a free lead-gen tool.
