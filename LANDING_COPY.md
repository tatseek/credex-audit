## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes, completely free. No credit card, no account required. You see your results instantly.

**Q: What do you do with my data?**
A: Your tool and spend data stays in your browser until you choose to share it via email. We never sell your data. If you submit your email, Credex may reach out for high-savings cases.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price in our audit engine is sourced from official vendor pricing pages and verified weekly. Sources are listed in our public PRICING_DATA.md file.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise and others — sourced from companies that overforecast or pivoted. The discount is real and substantial.

**Q: What if the tool says I am already spending well?**
A: We will tell you honestly. We do not manufacture fake savings. You can sign up to be notified when new optimizations apply to your stack.
